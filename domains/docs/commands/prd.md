Raw arguments: `$ARGUMENTS`

Choose `prd-light` for explicit light or quick requests, MVPs, early ideas, internal tools, or small-to-medium features. Choose `prd` for explicit full or formal requests and high-stakes, cross-team, security-sensitive, compliance-heavy, API-heavy, regulated, or approval-heavy work.

When the choice is clear, load and follow the selected skill immediately. If the request is missing or the choice is ambiguous, ask one plain-language triage question, then load and follow the answer. The selected skill owns the interview, draft, write step, and output; do not add another confirmation or restate its contract.
